Engineering Connections Events: 

Foodie Tuesday: Every Tuesday join the Engineering Connections professional and student staff for fun activities and free food in Williams Village East from 5pm-7pm. Each week is a different culinary theme and activity which give you the opportunity to learn about different resources and offices on campus: cultural centers & student orgs, engineering resources like the libraries and alumni, engineering ProReady career services, and more! This is where Engineering Connections promotes a lot of other fun and educational opportunities, don't miss out!
APPM Practice Exams: The Wednesday before each APPM Calc 1 & PreCalc midterm, the Engineering Academic Success team will host a practice exam. These are special copies of the exam which will give you a real-life experience of what the exam will be like! We hear from students that calculus exams at CU Boulder are much different than other exams they have experienced in high school or even in other college-level coursework. Just like the real exam, you will gather at 6:30pm, in a large group, in an exam-taking environment so you can get your brain and body used to this new experience. Each practice exam is followed by a small-group experience where students can get help on problems they missed and suggestions on what they might need to study before the real exam the next week. 
Post Midterm Snacks: Midterms are hard! Come relax and recuperate in the Williams Village East lobby after each Calc 1/Pre Calc midterm. The Engineering Connections team will have fun activities and food ready to help calm your body and mind. Academic Coaches and Mary Rader (the Student Support & Wellness Coordinator) will also be around should you want to process and talk about how your exam went. 
Learning Stations: The academic success team and upper division students will visit the Williams Village North lobby every few weeks to offer timely tips and activities to help elevate your learning and task management systems.See the schedule and other academic support resources here. You can stop by, learn some helpful tips & strategies, ask questions about learning, studying, time management, and beyond!
