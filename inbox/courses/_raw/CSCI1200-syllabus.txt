--- PAGE: Professional Academic Coaching ---
Professional Academic Coaches (PAC) in CEAS are professionally-trained staff members, often with a certification or credential in coaching, who develop and maintain in-depth knowledge of strategies that support effective learning and executive functioning, while achieving and maintaining balance.

Participating in Professional Academic Coaching allows CEAS students to reflect, discover, and plan within a series of meetings using a coaching approach and framework.

Students typically meet with their PAC at least four times throughout one semester.

PACs facilitate each session to help students reflect on their current situation and set goals they want to achieve.

The PAC and student work together to co-create a plan of action for moving forward and generating positive and lasting change.

Meet one-on-one with a Professional Academic Coach dedicated to engineering students, either both in-person and remotely!

Please visit CEAS Academic Coaching for more information.
