### Kant reading notes (Thought Project #1 prep)

- **Enlightenment** = emergence from *self-imposed* immaturity (inability to use your own understanding without a guide).
- **Motto:** *Sapere aude!* — “Have courage to use your own understanding!”
- **Why people stay immature:** laziness + cowardice; “guardians” (books, pastors, physicians) make thinking for yourself look hard/dangerous.
- **Individual vs public:** hard alone; a *public* can enlighten itself if given freedom — but slowly, or it may reject reformers.

**Prompt angle for your reflection:** What is *your* philosophy of higher education in engineering/IBE terms — where do you still rely on “guardians” vs thinking for yourself? Connect Kant to CU/Leeds/CEAS experience if useful.
