### #8 study notes (from capture)

- **8(a)** $\sqrt{27x^2y^8}$ → factor perfect squares: $3\sqrt{3}\,x\,y^4$
- **8(b)** $(16b)^{1/2}(9b^{1/4})$ → $4b^{1/2}\cdot 9b^{1/4}=36b^{3/4}$
- **8(c)** $(4r^{16}s^{-1/2})^{1/2}(32s^{-5/4})^{-1/5}$ — work each factor separately, then multiply:
  - $(4r^{16}s^{-1/2})^{1/2}=2r^8 s^{-1/4}$
  - $(32s^{-5/4})^{-1/5}=32^{-1/5}s^{1/4}=\tfrac12 s^{1/4}$ (since $32=2^5$)
  - Product: $2r^8 s^{-1/4}\cdot \tfrac12 s^{1/4}=r^8$

**Pitfall on 8(c):** treat each parenthesis with power-of-a-power separately; watch negative exponents on $s$; $32^{1/5}=2$, not 32.
