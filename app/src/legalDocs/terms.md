## What ProductName does not do on Canvas

ProductName **does not** submit assignments, post comments, or post or reply in discussions on your behalf. Those tools stay preview-only. You complete the action in Canvas.

It does not automate Bucket-B assessment tools (WebAssign, ZyBooks, PlayPosit, proctored exams). You operate those in the browser.

## Email and calendar writes

Personal Gmail send and Google Calendar event create/update run only after you confirm a specific preview for that action (ConfirmationGuard). There is no standing or automatic send. This round of the app stores a **local** calendar file; Google Calendar writes are a documented seam, not an automatic path.
