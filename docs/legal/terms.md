## 2. What the App is

The App is a **local-first desktop study tool**. It signs in to your school's Canvas with **your own single sign-on session**, reads the courses, assignments, quizzes, pages and syllabus text you can already see, and stores that data **on your computer** so it can draw your semester, help you plan for exams, and run short practice sessions.

The App is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to** the University of Colorado Boulder, any other school, or Instructure (the maker of Canvas). Canvas is a trademark of Instructure, Inc.

## 3. What the App will never do in Canvas

The App **reads** Canvas. It does **not** act toward anyone else on your behalf. Specifically, it never:

- submits or resubmits an assignment or quiz;
- posts, replies to, or edits a discussion;
- comments on a submission or messages an instructor or classmate;
- takes a quiz, exam, or any assessment for you, in Canvas or in any tool linked from Canvas (WebAssign, zyBooks, PlayPosit, proctored exams and similar).

Any "submit", "post" or "comment" feature is preview-only: it can show you a draft, and that is all. There is no setting, plan or upgrade that turns preview into execution.

## 4. Email and calendar

If you connect your **personal** Gmail or Google Calendar, the App can send an email or create/update a calendar event **only after** it shows you the exact content and you confirm that specific action in that moment. There is no automatic, scheduled or standing send/create mode, and none can be enabled. Reading, drafting and suggesting never authorise sending. Apple Calendar is not supported.
