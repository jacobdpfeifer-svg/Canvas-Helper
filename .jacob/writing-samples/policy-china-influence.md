# Policy — China influence / national security thesis

**Genre:** Formal policy / long persuasive

## Excerpt (opening + roadmap + analogy)

“The eventual demise of capitalism and the ultimate victory of socialism will require a long historical process to reach completion,” President Xi Jinping said in a 2019 statement (FBI). Xi uses capitalism and socialism to reflect two nations: the United States and the People's Republic of China. President Xi and his Communist Party seek to crush capitalism. Americans are at risk. Livelihoods are at risk. National security is at risk. China believes that they will be the global superpower and desire to displace the U.S. from dominance.

Past precedent exhibits China cheats, steals, and lies to obtain critical information, bolstering their geopolitical and tactical positions. America's current strategy does little to protect the U.S. China has demonstrated their plan clearly: world domination. Americans seek the protection of capitalism and democracy. The United States must modify its national security strategy towards China in regards to two key sectors: technology and trade.

Envision a delivery truck that supplies food to a town; its citizens are reliant on the truck making daily deliveries. Now imagine that the truck suddenly discovers a mechanism that prevents it from transporting the goods to the population. Malware has been planted on the truck by those who want to harm the town. The city flounders as they discover they have no other option; the enemy has absolute control over their food source. Imagine this food is the electricity that the U.S. feeds through its power grid. Unfortunately, this situation is not a figment of imagination. The “delivery truck” that supplies American electricity is in danger.

## Excerpt (call to action)

Every moment, the technology that America has developed, implemented and relies upon is endangered. If action is not taken, the grip China holds over the U.S. will tighten. It's only a matter of time before China makes a move that America is not ready for. Free and open market complacency has characterized the American viewpoint for long enough. Change must come with no subtleties but harsh and drastic measures. Protectionism is a virtue that every country should maintain. By no means should protectionism be the only policy, but economic balance must be a fundamental element of sound governance.

There is a need to radically change. There is a need to thwart the dominance China has over America. Reform begins with a change in the products consumers purchase, legislation protecting and promoting domestic markets, removal and replacement of infrastructure that allows China control, bolstering our presence geopolitically, and developing our own pathway to influencing other countries in a free and fair, equally beneficial relationship. This is the call to action. This is the plea for change. Alleviating Chinese dominance matters to anyone and everyone who seeks to enjoy existing, robust American freedom.
