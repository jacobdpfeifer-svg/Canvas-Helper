# Essay stance — Shein ethical review

**Genre:** Ethics / short research  
**Why Jacob:** Consumer scenario hook; utilitarianism + virtue; personal line (“I have never purchased…”); values close with call to examine before checkout.

## Excerpt (opening)

If you saw a brand new pair of jeans for under 15 dollars including shipping would you make the purchase? The other jeans you have been looking for are well above 80 dollars and unfortunately that does not include tax or shipping. You might be awestruck a company could sell jeans valued at 80 dollars for under 15 dollars. Most clothing companies would lose money selling at these costs but for the Chinese clothing brand Shein it is their business model. The company specializes in a new type of style called fast fashion. Using AI to scour the internet allows the organization to stay ahead of trends. With smart research Shein lists between 2,000 and 10,000 items daily. Customers will never have a lack of options when browsing, keeping them hooked to continue purchasing. It's clear that powerful marketing, rock bottom costs, and AI has caused the brand's popularity to skyrocket.

Is there a point where Shein’s practices become unethical? No one can outrightly say yes or no. Ethics are not universal which is why we need to look through the lens of utilitarianism.

## Excerpt (close)

I have never purchased anything from Shein nor do I intend to in the future. A company should not overwork employees, pollute the world, and emit high concentrations of greenhouse gases in the name of affordable clothing. If Shein was a person would their character be virtuous or ethical? Does the company employ compassion as a core business practice? Time and time again Shein has chosen to put profits before people. Is that a character driven trait? I don't think so. People are more valuable than profits. Human life is more valuable than any currency, notoriety, or accolade. Overall, the company fails on the moral lenses. Whether you decide to buy from Shein is up to your moral compass. My hope is that the next time your eyes fall upon the trendy jeans for far under MSRP you put them under the harsh light of critical examination and ponder if it is ethical to click checkout.
